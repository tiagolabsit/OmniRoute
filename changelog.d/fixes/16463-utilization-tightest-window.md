One exhausted account no longer paints a multi-account provider as 0% and the series no longer flat-lines on a slow weekly window Bucket timestamps are now ISO-8601 with `Z` (they were bare UTC strings that browsers read as local time, shifting every chart label by the viewer's UTC offset).
